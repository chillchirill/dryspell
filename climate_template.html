About these numbers
Each square shows how often it has a fire seen by satellite (NASA VIIRS) on a day of that month, averaged over the last two years. It is the usual pattern of the season, not a weather forecast. Two years show the rhythm of the year and a first look at change; they are too short for a long climate trend.
